The Ambient Workspace YouTube projector is an **independent lightweight adaptation** of upstream `src/scripts/libs/projector-2d.js` and `projector-shadow.js` concepts (multiple progressively scaled video projections and edge-fade). It additionally stretches real source-frame edge strips and draws corners. **It is not a drop-in copy of the complete upstream projector, and does not include upstream WebGL, bar detection, banding or options engine.** Chrome branding and original artwork are not included.

The original code remains copyright its original author. Please preserve these notices if distributing a modified build.
